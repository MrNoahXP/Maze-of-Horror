The Endless Maze

You wake in the dark. Ten phases stand between you and daylight. Each one hides something that hunts. Move with WASD, look with the mouse, hold Shift to sprint, press F for your flashlight, Space to dodge, and Left Click to strike when it finds you.

Prototype build — Phases 1–3 fully playable. Architecture supports Phases 4–10 (see source comments).
